How these figures are worked out
  • Cost per payer = spend entered for the campaign in the period ÷ its payers. Organic and referral have no entered spend, so no cost.
  • Net per month = estimated net income of the payers (Google Play prices, VAT and Google's fee taken out, refunds deducted) ÷ months they paid for.
  • Churn = payers whose paid time ran out ÷ payer-months observed. Months paying = 1 ÷ churn, capped at {{ d.lifetimeCapMonths }}.
  • Lifetime value = net per month × months paying. Payback = cost per payer ÷ net per month. Rule of thumb: value ÷ cost of 3× or more is healthy; below 1× the strategy loses money.
  • With few payers every one of these moves a lot. They only stop being flagged provisional from {{ d.minPayers }} payers, and old cohorts are worth more than recent ones.
