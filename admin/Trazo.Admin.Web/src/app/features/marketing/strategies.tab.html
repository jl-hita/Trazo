How to read these numbers
  • Attribution. A person counts in one strategy only: an invitation code wins over a campaign, a campaign wins over organic. The campaign is known when the account is created, from the link the person tapped before installing from Google Play.
  • Clicks and installs. Clicks are link openings (link-preview robots excluded). Installs are first launches where the app read the campaign. People who install from elsewhere, or on another device, show up as organic.
  • Conversion is measured on the accounts created in the period. A payer is someone whose first real payment was confirmed by Google Play; free trials, test purchases and promotional time never count.
  • Confidence. With few signups, a rate swings a lot. Below {{ d.minReliable }} signups a strategy is flagged "small sample"; use the range shown under each rate before deciding anything.
  • Cost. Spend is what you entered per campaign day in the period. Estimated revenue is the net income of those customers so far (renewals included, refunds deducted), computed from the Google Play prices of the tax settings, VAT and Google's fee already taken out. It is orientative: the real figures are in Google Play Console.
  • Retention. "Still training" means the person logged a workout in the week that starts 30 days after signing up. Only people old enough to have had that week are counted.
